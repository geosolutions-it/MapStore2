What are Cookies?

A cookie is a text file that a website saves on the browser of the user's computer. Cookies usually allow the site to remember the preferences expressed by the user, so that they don't need to be entered again later. The browser stores the information and sends it back to the site's server the next time the user visits that website.

Our technical cookies help us to:

  • Make the website work as expected
  • Remember settings during and between visits to the site
  • Keep the session active during authentication
  • Improve the security of the site

The site does not use cookies to:

  • Collect personally identifiable information for profiling purposes
  • Collect special categories of data (the so-called sensitive data)
  • Pass data to advertising networks
  • Pass personal data to third parties for marketing purposes
  • Send unsolicited commercial communications

You can find out more about all the cookies we use below.

Technical cookies are installed automatically, as they are necessary for the site to function, and do not require your consent. If you disable them, our site may not work as you would expect.
