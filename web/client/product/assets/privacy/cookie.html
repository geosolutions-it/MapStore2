About cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. LocalStorage and SessionStorage are separate browser storage mechanisms. External requests and cookie handling

OSM Bright is served by six GeoSolutions WMS hosts. It is distinct from tile.openstreetmap.org, which is used only when that basemap is selected. A request to a map service is not evidence of an optional tracking purpose. The WMS emits GS_FLOW_CONTROL even if a browser blocks its storage; a blocked cookie is still an emitted cookie. Whether the browser stores or sends it also depends on browser settings. MapStore cannot delete cookies belonging to another domain.

Map imagery, geocoding searches and catalogue operations send connection metadata and requested coordinates, text or resources to their destination. The baseline checks did not observe cookies from the separate public OSM tile service, the Nominatim search service or the configured catalogue and added WMS layer. Retention and withdrawal limits

The preference record uses a configured 180-day lifetime, distinct from provider cookie lifetimes and from any in-memory concurrency timeout in GeoServer. Read access, consent updates, focus and periodic checks validate it. Withdrawal prevents further work by registered integrations and requests cancellation where supported. Requests already transmitted, browser caches, remote logs and cookies controlled by other domains cannot be erased by MapStore. Use browser privacy controls for third-party storage.
